    • MAIN TERMINALS

  • CN (Canadian National Railway)

    • CN Brampton Intermodal Terminal is a major rail logistics facility operated by Canadian National Railway in Brampton, Ontario. It serves as a key hub for transferring freight containers between trucks and trains, supporting efficient intermodal transportation across Canada and into the United States. The terminal handles import and export containers, container storage, and rail-to-truck distribution, helping streamline supply chain operations for shippers and carriers.

  • CP (Canadian Pacific Railway)

    • CP Vaughan Intermodal Terminal is a large freight logistics facility operated by Canadian Pacific Kansas City in Vaughan, Ontario. The terminal is used to transfer shipping containers between trains and trucks, supporting intermodal transportation across Canada, the United States, and Mexico. It handles import and export containers, container storage, and rail-to-truck distribution, helping improve the efficiency of supply chain and freight movement.

    OTHER TERMINALS

  • AMAR TRANSPORT

    • AMAR Transport is a Canadian-owned transportation and logistics company. Their Brampton terminal serves as a central hub for their fleet and freight operations, strategically positioned to access major 400-series highways (like the 401 and 407).

  • P&W

    • P&W Intermodal (a division of TFI International) is a major container transport specialist operating heavily in the Brampton and Toronto rail hubs.

  • MUSKET

    • Musket Transport is one of the largest asset-based intermodal carriers in Ontario. While they are heavily active in Brampton (especially at the CN rail terminal), their physical headquarters and primary yards are located in Mississauga.

  • BEST CHOICE

    • Best Choice Terminal is a logistics and container handling facility commonly used in freight and shipping operations. It plays an important role in the movement, storage, and management of containers between ports, rail yards, and trucking companies..

  • SEAPORT

    • A seaport is a facility located on the coast where ships load and unload cargo. It serves as a key gateway for international trade and transportation.
